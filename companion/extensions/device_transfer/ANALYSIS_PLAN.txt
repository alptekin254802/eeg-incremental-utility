QUESTION
Does the EEG increment retain its direction when all training participants were
recorded with one device and all assessment participants with the other?
Existing device covariate adjustment and device-by-group loss summaries use
mixed-device training. They do not answer this train/test separation question.

COHORT AND EXCLUSIONS
Use the same 96 retained participants and GROUP-derived binary outcome. No new
exclusions or relabeling. Two exhaustive directions: EPOC X training (54; 16 ADHD,
38 control) -> EPOC+ assessment (42; 25 ADHD,17 control), and reverse. These counts
are already public in the current manuscript; no new outcome results were used.
Use frozen participant features, covariance matrices, QC device labels and
outcome linkage. Do not re-extract raw EEG or download any dataset.

MODELS AND COMPARISONS
Three procedures in each direction: seven-variable non-EEG baseline; original
spectral/covariance selector (regional spectral, expanded spectral, covariance);
fixed nine-feature permutation-entropy/Hjorth augmentation. Comparisons are each
EEG procedure minus the same baseline under the original oriented differences.
Keep all five fixed representation fits (baseline, three selector candidates,
entropy/Hjorth), all tuning scores and predictions. No new representation search.
L2 logistic regression, C={0.01,0.1,1,10,100}, no class weights, original solver,
tolerance and convergence retry. Five stratified inner folds within the source
device only; shuffle seed 20261101 for X source, 20261102 for Plus source.
Pooled inner participant log loss selects C; ties rounded to 12 decimals favor
smaller C. The spectral/covariance selector also favors regional, expanded,
covariance in that order for tied scores. All scaling, PCA and covariance
references fit on each training subset; final fit uses all source-device rows.
No assessment-device feature adaptation, target intercept recalibration, or use
of assessment labels in selection. A device indicator is constant in training
and is not included. Do not fit additional subgroups or tune to these results.

METRICS AND UNCERTAINTY
Primary descriptive metric: delta LL=baseline LL - augmented LL; positive favors
augmentation. Also retain AUROC difference (augmented-baseline), Brier difference
(baseline-augmented), absolute metrics, target group-specific mean loss changes,
and the equal-class average of the two group mean loss changes. The last is a
fixed-prediction prevalence-weighting diagnostic, not target recalibration.
For the four principal direction/procedure contrasts, use 5000 paired target-row
bootstrap draws, stratified by GROUP, seed 20261103. Save all draws. Report 2.5th
and 97.5th percentiles (linear interpolation) as descriptive conditional ranges,
holding source fitting, tuning and target class counts fixed. They do NOT cover
source-training/model-selection uncertainty or device-to-device variability; no
hypothesis tests, p values or confirmatory coverage claims. Do not average the
two directions into a putative independent-device estimate.

PREDEFINED AUDIT AND STOPPING
Expect 250 inner fits (2 directions x 5 models x 5 C x 5 folds), plus 10 final
fixed-model fits. Preserve every candidate result, including poor outcomes.
Check disjoint source/assessment IDs, inner nesting, class availability, finite
inputs, convergence, prediction range, metric/sign identities and source hashes.
Before fitting, hash this plan and all input/code dependencies. Afterward verify
original hashes unchanged. If a candidate fails, record the exact failure and
stop for a documented technical correction; do not silently discard candidates.
One deterministic replay of the new small analysis is allowed only as a
reproducibility check, not selection of seeds. No expanded experiment campaign.

INTERPRETATION AND INTEGRATION RULE (BEFORE RESULTS)
This is a stress test of transfer between two observed device-associated groups.
Device, participant composition/prevalence and reduced training sample size
change together. Poor transfer does not establish a device-caused artifact;
good transfer does not establish independent-cohort or unseen-device validity.
Primary manuscript result is unchanged whatever this experiment finds.
Keep the complete family in the supplement with one compact table or figure.
Add a short main-text finding and limitation if the experiment is valid. Do not
promote it to a separate primary discovery or add it to the abstract merely for
favorable results. Match interpretation to both directions and all metrics.

OTHER CANDIDATES CONSIDERED
Participant heterogeneity, leverage of extreme losses, conditional resampling,
quality/device covariates, EEG block ablations, matched-dimensional spectral
controls and no-EEG selection already exist. Do not rerun as new requirements.
Full training bootstrap would address another uncertainty component, but is not
necessary for this bounded transfer question and is not undertaken here.
No raw temporal behavior/EEG mechanism reconstruction, new neural architecture,
clinical decision threshold/net benefit, or diagnostic relabeling: unsupported
by the current measurement/label scope or not needed for the central question.
