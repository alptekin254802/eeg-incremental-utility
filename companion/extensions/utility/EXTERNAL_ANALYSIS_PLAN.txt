Independent adult-data extension: analysis plan, 1 November 2026

Status and knowledge at planning
This is a new post-hoc analysis, not part of the original BALLADEER preregistration. The published Wiebe et al. result and its selected model containing no EEG features are known. Before locking this plan, only the published article, public repository metadata, variable dictionary, CSV headers and authors' scripts were examined. No new predictions or outcome-associated feature statistics from these CSVs have been computed. The source article reports 50 training and 36 independent test participants. The source is https://osf.io/ewkgv/ (DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/EWKGV), CC BY 4.0 according to its official API license metadata. Original downloaded bytes and hashes are preserved.

Question and estimand
Does adding the released spectral EEG features improve held-out probability prediction beyond task performance and eye tracking in a separately collected adult ADHD dataset? This is a conceptual replication with newly trained models and different features, task, age group and ascertainment, NOT external validation of a frozen BALLADEER model and NOT an enlargement of the pediatric cohort.

Participants and outcome
Use all rows in the authors' original training and test files, preserving the published split. Training Group HC=0, ADHD_NoMed=1; test Group HC=0, ADHD=1, as documented in the source scripts. Unexpected labels, infinite numeric values, or unidentified parsing errors cause a recorded stop. Do not exclude rows based on prediction results. Missing predictors are retained and imputed. Source row numbers are the only available local identifiers; do not claim independent identity verification. Check exact duplicate predictor rows across files as an integrity diagnostic, not a reason for silent exclusion.

Predictors and comparison family
Primary adult comparison: all CPT_ and ET_ columns as baseline (expected 14 task + 35 eye-tracking predictors); add all EEG_ columns (expected 18 theta/alpha/beta-power measures at Fz/Cz/Pz in distractor and non-distractor phases).
One fixed broader-baseline sensitivity: all released non-EEG predictors (also Acti_ and ES_; expected 58) versus the same baseline plus the 18 EEG predictors (76 total). This changes the baseline information set and is not a second independent replication. No demographics are released in these feature files. No feature selection, feature-ranking or alternative subset search will be added.

Model family and training
Use all three preselected learners from the empirical extension: L2 logistic regression, RBF SVM and random forest, with the exact grids in code/run_extension.py and ANALYSIS_PLAN.txt. Within the 50-person training sample use five stratified inner folds, shuffle seed 20261131. For every inner training partition separately: drop columns entirely missing in that partition, fit median imputation on remaining training columns, and fit standard scaling on the imputed training values. Apply these fitted transforms to inner validation data. Select the grid candidate by pooled inner log loss (round to 12 decimal places only for tie handling, then grid order). Refit transforms and model on all training participants, and apply them unchanged to the independent test file. SVM probability calibration is internal to each training fit, as in the empirical extension. Model seeds 20261133+inner_fold and 20261232 for the final fit. Source authors' preprocessing and selection scripts are inspected as documentation and will not be executed. Total planned explicit fit calls: 2 baselines x 2 models x (26+31+16) = 292, excluding SVM's internal probability calibration.

Metrics and uncertainty
Primary metric is paired test log-loss increment, baseline minus EEG model (clip probabilities at 1e-8). Secondary AUROC increment is EEG minus baseline; Brier increment is baseline minus EEG. Report both members' absolute metrics, all six paired comparisons, group-specific mean log-loss changes and participant-level paired changes. No inferential success/failure threshold and no best-learner claim.
For each fixed fitted pair, use the same 10,000 paired stratified test-participant bootstrap samples (seed 20261137), resampling within the observed test classes and retaining their counts. Save all resampled metric increments and the 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. These are descriptive conditional ranges: training, preprocessing/model selection, population prevalence, and choice of this dataset are held fixed. They are not full-pipeline confidence intervals or confirmatory tests. Do not refit inside this resampling.

Integrity and integration
Record missingness by split and feature, dropped columns by training partition, split membership, all candidate/fold scores, chosen models, test probabilities, metrics, and hashes. Check training-transformation invariance when test predictors are perturbed, verify paired metrics independently from saved probabilities, and repeat the deterministic final prediction computation once. Preserve original public files and original manuscript/results unchanged. Keep all outcomes regardless of sign. Integrate a compact account in the main text and complete methods/results in the supplement; retain the pediatric primary question and analysis unchanged. Do not pool adult and pediatric rows or directly compare their absolute discrimination as a contest. Source ethics (Bonn protocols 113/21 and 297/20, written informed consent) apply to the original adult studies; no new ethics decision is asserted for this secondary analysis.
