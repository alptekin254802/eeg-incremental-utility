Targeted exploratory sensitivity analyses - 3 November 2026

Status and timing
Written after the original and subsequent results were known, before computing
the new transformations, fits, and outcomes below. This is a dated exploratory
analysis plan, not part of the original preregistration. It responds to specific
alternative explanations without selecting a successful new prediction model.

Fixed sample and reference
The same 96 BALLADEER participants, GROUP target (41/55), seven baseline inputs,
original ten-by-five outer and five inner partitions, original learned
transformations, L2 logistic solver, C grid [.01,.1,1,10,100], clipping rules and
selection tie rules are retained. No new exclusions, labels or imputation.
All archived numerical outputs and scientific code remain read-only.

A. Oracle prior-odds diagnostic for device transfer (no predictive refitting)
Question: how does the paired EEG increment change after a common prior-odds
correction, and can a simple probability shift improve the baseline's loss?
Use both saved transfer directions and the spectral/covariance selector and
entropy/Hjorth comparisons (four principal comparisons). For each direction add
logit(observed target GROUP fraction)-logit(observed source GROUP fraction) to
each saved baseline AND augmented logit. This uses target labels intentionally
as an oracle diagnostic. Retain original and corrected probabilities; report
absolute log loss, AUROC, Brier, paired increments, group-specific losses,
observed-composition and equal-class increments, and mean predicted probability.
No optimized offset or additional recalibration. Paired stratified target
resampling (5000 draws, seed 2026110301) holds class counts, prior offset and source
fits fixed. Report descriptive conditional percentile ranges, not full-procedure
confidence intervals. Corrections rely on a prior-shift interpretation that need
not hold across devices. Neither improved loss nor attenuation identifies a
device-mediated mechanism or an implementable target-label-free predictor.

B. Nonlinear age sensitivity (new frozen-feature fits)
Question: do the primary and entropy/Hjorth increments depend on a linear age
term in the baseline? Replace age with a restricted cubic spline with fixed
knots [6,10,14,18] years, giving age and two nonlinear columns (three degrees of
freedom). Fixed knots use the stated pediatric age range, not outcome-guided
choices. No age-by-sex interaction or alternate knot search.
Retain sex and all task/gaze inputs. Add identical age columns to each paired
baseline and EEG model. Evaluate exactly three comparisons: unadjusted primary
spectral/covariance selector; unadjusted entropy/Hjorth; device-plus-quality
adjusted entropy/Hjorth. The adjusted comparison retains all three original
acquisition covariates. Scaling, PCA, covariance reference and C/representation
selection remain within each training partition. Keep all candidate and inner
scores, probabilities, selections and all repeats. The comparison tests a fixed
alternative demographic model, not causal age adjustment or global optimality.

C. One bandwidth sensitivity (raw-derived feature extraction and new fits)
Question: does entropy/Hjorth augmentation persist after stronger attenuation of
frequencies above 30 Hz? Reconstruct the original preprocessed continuous
segments and accepted window IDs from the existing local raw files. First verify
original QC and unfiltered E0/E4 features against the archived values. Apply one
additional zero-phase fourth-order Butterworth low-pass at 30 Hz (SOS) to each
already preprocessed 1-40 Hz continuous segment at 128 Hz, then reuse exactly
the original accepted 256-sample windows. Filtering does not cross gaps. Do not
perform another rejection pass, change interpolation or participant eligibility.
Recompute the same nine entropy/Hjorth summaries (m=5, tau=1, original finite
differences and median aggregation). Evaluate unadjusted and device-plus-quality
adjusted comparisons, each against its unchanged matched baseline. No other
cutoff, aperiodic fit, added spectral band or representation selector is tried.
Retain all extracted features and both comparisons regardless of direction.
This is sensitivity to an additional filter, including its transition and edge
effects; it cannot identify muscle, device or physiological signal origin.

Metrics, uncertainty and accounting for B/C
Primary descriptive metric: participant-level held-out log loss, averaged within
each repeat then across repeats; delta = baseline minus augmented loss. AUROC and
Brier are secondary, oriented to favor EEG. Record all ten repeat estimates,
median/IQR/range and positive-repeat count. Compare the new paired increments
with their unchanged original reference increments. For the five planned paired
comparisons and changes relative to references, resample the 96 participant mean
loss differences with replacement 5000 times (seed 2026110302, common draws).
Ranges are conditional descriptions with predictions and fitting fixed, not
training/selection uncertainty or confirmatory inference. No p-values, repeat-
independence assumption, result-dependent removal or stopping.

Validation and retention
Verify source identities and exact nested split membership, check training-only
designs by held-out perturbations, and replay original baseline/E4 predictions
to numerical tolerance 1e-7. Raw re-extraction must reproduce original features
to 1e-8 and accepted-window counts. Test spline continuity/linear tails and
prior-shift algebra on synthetic inputs. An implementation failure is logged;
fixes are dated and justified before rerunning the affected operation. No change
to hypotheses, model family or cutoff in response to predictive outcomes.
All family results are saved in a separate addendum. Integrate by relevance,
primarily in a supplementary section and a short main-text interpretation.

External-data feasibility review
Assess primary documentation for independent pediatric raw EEG with aligned
behavior, gaze, age/sex and defensible ADHD/control ascertainment. Record actual
release contents, task/channel compatibility, permissions and missing fields.
Separate a frozen pediatric model's external validation from a new matched
sensor-addition conceptual replication. No external test is claimed without
obtained, permitted, compatible inputs. No bulk download or access application
is part of this feasibility review.
